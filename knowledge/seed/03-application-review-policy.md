# Application review policy

## Why an application is rejected

An application is rejected when the document checklist is incomplete, the identity document is expired, the legal name does not match the application, the proof of address is older than 90 days, or an eligibility question was left unanswered. An application is also rejected when a prior account is in bad standing and the notice says so. A rejection is not a permanent ban unless the notice explicitly says fraud.

## What to check before reapplying

Before reapplying, wait 14 days, correct every item named in the rejection notice, include the previous application reference, and complete the document checklist. Do not open a duplicate application while an earlier application is still in review. Review of a complete application takes 5 business days. A support ticket can track a reapplication question, and the ticket priority for that request is normal.
