# Escalation policy

A case should be escalated when a support ticket already exists and the case missed the published review window or contradicts written policy. Escalate a case only after a support ticket exists. Email alone does not escalate an application review. Escalation is for cases that missed the published support window or contradict the written policy, not for skipping the document checklist. Customers do not assign urgent priority. Urgent handling is not a customer-selectable option in this support process.
